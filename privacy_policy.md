# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 20 September, 2026

This is the privacy policy for Minigames Ahoy!, a Discord game bot. Here's what we collect, why, and how you can get rid of it.

## What we store

Just two things:

- **Your Discord User ID** — the random number Discord assigns your account. Not your username, avatar, email, or anything else tied to your Discord profile.
- **Your game data** — your level, wins/losses/games played, coins, and similar stats from playing the bot's games.

That's it. No messages, no voice data, no IP addresses, nothing else.

## Why we store it

So the bot can remember your progress between sessions — your level, your stats, your coins, that kind of thing. Some of this may also show up on leaderboards. We don't use your data for ads or sell it to anyone.

## Where it lives

Your data sits in a database tied to your Discord ID. Only the people running the bot have access, and only to keep things working and fix bugs.

## Who else sees it

Nobody, basically. The only exceptions are our database host (who just stores the data, doesn't look at it) and situations where we're legally required to hand something over.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you keep using the bot, or until you delete it yourself. We don't have an automatic expiration on inactive accounts right now.

## Deleting your data

Run `/profile settings` in the bot and pick the delete option. This wipes your Discord ID and all your game data — level, coins, stats, everything — for good. There's no undo, so make sure that's what you want.

## Age requirement

You need to meet Discord's own age minimum (13, or higher if your country requires it) to use the bot. We're not set up to knowingly collect data from anyone younger than that.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, we'll post about it in this GitHub repository. Using the bot after that means you're okay with the update.
