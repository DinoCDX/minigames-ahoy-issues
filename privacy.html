Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This is the privacy policy for Minigames Ahoy!, a Discord game bot. Here's what we collect, why, and how you can get rid of it.

What we store

  • Your Discord user ID: the number Discord assigns your account. Not your username, avatar, email or anything else tied to your Discord profile.
  • Your game data: your level, wins, losses, games played, fishing data and similar stats from playing the bot's games.
  • Server settings: if a server's admins configure the bot, we store that server's ID and the IDs of the channels chosen for features such as counting or question of the day.
  • Quotes: when someone quotes a message with the quote command, the quote is stored. You can delete quotes made of you.

We don't store voice data, IP addresses or your private messages.

Why we store it

So the bot can remember your progress between sessions and keep each server's settings working. Some game data may show up on leaderboards. We don't use your data for ads or sell it to anyone.

Where it lives

Your data sits in a database tied to your Discord ID or server ID. Only the people running the bot have access, and only to keep things working and fix bugs.

Who else sees it

Nobody, basically. The only exceptions are our database host, which stores the data but doesn't look at it, and situations where we're legally required to hand something over.

How long we keep it

  • User data: as long as you keep using the bot, or until you delete it yourself. There is no automatic expiry for inactive accounts right now.
  • Server data: deleted automatically 7 days after the bot is removed from the server.

Deleting your data

Run /profile settings and pick the delete option. This permanently wipes your Discord ID and all your game data, including your level, stats and fishing data. There is no undo, so make sure that's what you want. Quotes made of you can also be deleted.

Age requirement

You must meet Discord's minimum age (13, or higher if your country requires it) to use the bot. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll post about it in the GitHub repository. Using the bot after that means you accept the update.
